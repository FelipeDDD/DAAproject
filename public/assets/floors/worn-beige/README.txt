USAGE
-----
Clean, Worn and Dirty are intended to work well as repeating base floors.

Cracked and Stained are detail variations. For more natural-looking environments,
use them occasionally among the base floor tiles rather than repeating them across
an entire room.

All textures are designed to tile seamlessly on all four sides.

The assets can be used in games, prototypes, animations, illustrations and other
personal or commercial projects, subject to the included LICENSE.txt.

AI DISCLOSURE
-------------
This is an AI-assisted asset pack. Generative AI was used in the creation of the
source graphics. The selected assets were subsequently curated, processed,
color-matched, resized and tested for seamless repetition.
